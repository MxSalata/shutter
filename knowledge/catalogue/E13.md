# E13 An event time the receiver's record can't hold

## Meaning

The failed operation's event time doesn't fit the encounter: it is before the patient's admission, more than five
minutes after the message was sent (MSH-7) or the evidence was taken, more than a day from the time the PAS recorded
the event (EVN-2), or within the hour around a clock change where a local time without an offset can't be ordered; or a later event for the
encounter, applied or sent after the failed one, carries a time more than five minutes after it was sent, applied or
seen, and believing that time would decide the case (a later transfer or discharge; an update decides nothing).
Shutter orders and supersedes movements by event time, so a wrong time can resend a movement that predates the stay,
or make genuine failures look overtaken.

Such a time is not simply bad data, and the likely cause depends on the condition. A time more than a day before the
time the PAS recorded the event (EVN-2) is usually a retrospective entry: clinicians digitise paper records later, and
notes typed in more than a day after the event carry the genuine event time (EVN-6). A time in the future (of when the message says it was sent, of when the engine received it, or of the
evidence) or before the admission is usually a clock fault, in the PAS, the integration engine or the device that stamped the event, or a
mistyped time, in this event or in the admission. A local time without an offset within an hour of a clock change in the feed's zone may be one that night holds twice, when the clocks go back, or not at all, when they go forward, so only the source can
say when it was. Which of these it is, and whether the movement should still be sent, can't be told from the messages,
and Shutter doesn't correct the time: the case goes to a person with the times named, who decides with the PAS team
checking the source.

## Telling it apart

A movement overtaken by a later one with a sound time is E1; a failed movement still due is E2. Here the time itself
is the question. When the time is plausible but another source moved the patient within the site's `source_skew_seconds`, it is E7. A later
movement whose own time is sound supersedes (E1, E6); one timed in the future settles nothing and is this entry.

## Cite

The failed operation's event time, the admission time (PV1-44 or the receiver's first history entry), EVN-2, MSH-7, the
time the engine received the message, and the feed's time zone setting. For a later event: its event time beside the time it was sent (the timeline's
`sent_at`) or applied (the history's `applied_at`) and the time the evidence was taken.
