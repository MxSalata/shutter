# E11 A movement for an encounter the receiver doesn't know

## Meaning

A transfer or discharge failed, and the receiver answers 404 for its encounter: it has never seen the admission. Either
the admission was lost too, or the movement carries the wrong visit number. Resending the movement alone can only be
refused again, so the case goes to a person with the reason named.

## Telling it apart

A failed admission for an unknown encounter is E2: the admission is what creates the encounter. When the admission's
own failure is in the same incident, the two form one ordered chain, and the chain releases the admission first. A
receiver that can't be read (`unknown`, no answer) is not this entry: the state is unknown, not absent.

## Cite

The receiver's 404 for the encounter, the receipt lookup (404), and the timeline with no applied admission.
