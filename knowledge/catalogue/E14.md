# E14 A patient merge

## Meaning

The failed operation is an ADT^A40 merge: the patient named in PID takes over the history of each prior patient named
in an MRG segment. Under the site profile's `merges: forbidden`, the default, Shutter withholds every merge and sends
nothing, whatever the receiver answered, and the case goes to the PAS team named for the sending application. The
same holds when the site allows merges but the receiver's entry says it takes none.

## Telling it apart

An exact repeat of a merge the receiver already holds carries a receipt: that is E3's reconcile, not this entry. A
failed transfer or discharge for one of the merged patients is not a merge either; it follows the entries for its own
event.

## Cite

Every PATIENT group of the message (`prior_identifiers`, each with the patient it goes `into`), the receipt lookup, the
site profile's `merges` setting and the receiver's entry.
