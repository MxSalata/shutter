# E8 The receiver can't take a conditional write

## Meaning

The movement may well be due, but the receiver currently refuses requests with an expected version, or its capability
profile can't be read. Without that guard a resend is a blind write.

## Telling it apart

The route's capability profile (WIC-7) says `conditional_writes` is off, or it can't be read.

## Cite

The capability profile with its time, and what the evidence says about the movement itself.
