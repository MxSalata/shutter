# E2 Not delivered, still current

## Meaning

The receiver was down or unreachable when the movement was sent, holds no receipt for it, and nothing has happened to
the encounter since. The movement is still the patient's latest and is still due. An admission that never arrived is
this entry too: an admission is never superseded (ELS-2.4), and until it arrives the downstream system answers 404 for
the encounter (`not_found`). A receiver that can't be read at all (`unknown`) is not this entry: nothing is resent on a
state nobody could see.

## Telling it apart

Another visit of the same patient is not a later event. The gate reads it apart: a further in-patient movement on another
visit stops the resend and names both visits (`FURTHER_MOVEMENT`, S27) where the site profile says stop, as the demo's does, and
otherwise shows on the plan beside it; an out-patient attendance shows on the plan (S9). A rejection
(409 or 422) is not this entry; a duplicate answer without a receipt is E9. A later movement timed in the future is not
nothing later: the case is E13.

## Cite

The receipt lookup (404), the downstream system's current version and state, and the timeline with no later event.
