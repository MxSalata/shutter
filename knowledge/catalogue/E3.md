# E3 Committed after all

## Meaning

The answer was lost, not the movement: the receiver committed it and the acknowledgment never came back. Resending
would apply it twice.

## Telling it apart

Only the receipt decides (RP-3.1). A timeout or an error text says nothing either way. If the receipt lookup itself
is unavailable, this entry doesn't match: the receipt is `unreadable`, and that is no known pattern.

## Cite

The receipt (key, payload digest, version before and after) and the history entry for the operation.
