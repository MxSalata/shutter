# E6 The encounter is discharged

## Meaning

The patient has left. Whether the failed movement's event came before the discharge (it was overtaken) or after it
(it can't apply), the downstream system must not move a discharged patient.

## Telling it apart

The encounter's state is `discharged`. A failed discharge whose receipt exists is E3; a duplicate discharge for an
already discharged encounter closes here with nothing to send.

## Cite

The discharge from the downstream system's history (version, event time) and the failed operation's event time.
