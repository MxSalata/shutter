# E16 The destination bed is held by a patient who is leaving

## Meaning

The receiver understood the transfer or admission and said no: the bed it names is held by another patient's encounter.
The other patient is on the way out: their transfer or discharge out of that bed was sent but failed or is still in
another case, or it has been applied since the refusal. This is an ordering problem in disguise. Resending now fails
again; resending after the occupant's movement lands puts both patients where the source system says they are.

## Telling it apart

The failure's answer is `bed_occupied`, and the evidence's `bed_conflict` section finds a movement out of the bed for the
occupant (`occupant_leaving`: not applied yet, with the case about it when there is one; `occupant_left`: applied since the
refusal). When no movement out is known, or the occupant cannot be read, it is E17.

## What happens

The plan resends the case's own operation unchanged, conditional on this encounter's version, and waits on the occupant's
case. The gate holds it (`WAITING_FOR_OCCUPANT_CASE`) until that case resolves, passes it once the occupant has left
(`OCCUPANT_MOVED_OUT`), and escalates if that case ends without freeing the bed (`OCCUPANT_CASE_FAILED`). The receiver
refuses again if the bed is still held, so nothing is double-booked.

## Cite

The refusal (ward, bed, since), the occupant as the evidence names it (a token, never their identifiers), their movement
out with its delivery and case, and this encounter's current version.
