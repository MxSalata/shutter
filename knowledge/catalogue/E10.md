# E10 An update after the failed movement

## Meaning

After the movement failed, the patient's details were updated (an A08) and the update was applied. The update moved
nobody, so the failed movement is still due.

## Telling it apart

`later_applied` reads `update` only when no later transfer or discharge was applied; those take precedence (E1, E6).

## Cite

The update from the ward's history and the failed movement's receipt lookup (404).
