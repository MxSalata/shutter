# E19 A death on the message

## Meaning

The failed message's PID-29 or PID-30 records a death, or holds the HL7 null that clears one. The operation Shutter sends
carries neither field, so a resend would read as committed with the death not delivered, and a closure as overtaken would
leave it unread. A movement after a death may also be a real one, or a sign that the death is wrong. Which it is can't be
told from the messages, so this entry answers alone, whatever else matches: nothing is sent or closed, and the case goes to
a person who checks the patient with the team that owns the record.

## Telling it apart

Only the failed message's own fields are read. A death shown by an earlier message of the patient's isn't compared with
this one, and an N in PID-30 reads as no death: it says nothing about what the receiver held before.

## Cite

That the message records or clears a death (`death_recorded`, a word: the date stays in the message), the case's patient
and visit, and the receiver's current state.
