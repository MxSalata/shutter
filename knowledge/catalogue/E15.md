# E15 A cancellation the receiver cannot reverse

## Meaning

The failed operation cancels an earlier movement (ADT^A11, A12 or A13), and the receiver's entry in the site profile
says it takes no cancels. Shutter withholds the cancel and sends nothing: the receiver would refuse it, or apply it in
a way nobody can check. A person corrects the receiver by hand, then marks the case handled on the case page.

## Telling it apart

A receiver whose entry declares `cancels: true` takes the cancel, and this entry does not apply. A movement that a
later cancel already undid is E1: there is nothing left to resend.

## Cite

The cancel and the movement it names, the receiver's `cancels` setting, and the receiver's current state of the
encounter.
