# E17 Two patients are allocated to one bed

## Meaning

The source system has put two patients in one bed: the receiver holds one there, and the failed movement puts another in
the same bed, with nothing to say the first is leaving. That is a real clash on the ward, not a delivery fault. No write is
safe: a resend is refused again, and a receiver without the check would double-book the bed.

## Telling it apart

The failure's answer is `bed_occupied`, and the evidence's `bed_conflict` section finds no movement out of the bed for the
occupant (`occupant_staying`), or could not read the occupant (`unknown`). A known movement out is E16.

## What happens

The case escalates to the bed manager with the conflict laid out: the ward and bed, since when it has been held and by
which encounter (as the receiver names it), and the refused movement. The gate blocks any resend (`BED_OCCUPIED`). Once a
person frees or reallocates the bed, the case can be reinvestigated.

## Cite

The refusal (ward, bed, since), the occupant's encounter and its current placement, and that no movement out was found.
