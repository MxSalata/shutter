# E1 Superseded by a later transfer

## Meaning

The failed transfer was overtaken: the downstream system has applied another transfer for the same encounter whose event
happened later. The patient is where the later transfer put them, and the failed one describes a place they have already
left. When the later transfer has not reached the downstream system either (its delivery failed or is uncertain), the
PAS's later word on the patient's place still stands: its own case recovers it, and this one is closed with nothing to
send (later_delivery: sent).

## Telling it apart

Compare event times, not arrival times (WIC-1.2): the later transfer's event time must be strictly later. Equal times
are E7, not this. A later discharge is E6. A later demographic update is not a later movement (ELS-2.2, E10). A later
transfer whose time lies in the future of when it was sent or applied supersedes nothing: that is E13, and a person
checks the clock.

## Cite

The superseding event from the downstream system's history (version, ward, event time), or, when the receiver has not
applied it, the later message in the timeline (its event time, when it was sent and its outcome) and its case; and the
failed operation's event time.
