# E4 Several failures, in order

## Meaning

During an outage several movements of one encounter failed one after another, for example a transfer and then a
discharge, and nothing for the encounter was delivered between them. All of them are still due, in order.

## Telling it apart

No successful delivery for the encounter sits between the failures; otherwise the chain is interleaved (E5). Each item
goes at the version the previous item's verified receipt reports, and the chain stops at the first surprise.

## Cite

Each item's receipt lookup (404), the encounter's version before the first item, and the event times in order.
