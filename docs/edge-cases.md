# What can go wrong when you resend

A resend in a hospital can do harm with every check passed. This page lists ways it can go wrong and what Shutter does about each. **Seen in practice** marks the ones the author has met at hospitals. **Handled**: a test shows the outcome. **Stops and flags**: nothing is written and the case says why, so a person decides. Stopping is a right answer here, because the alternative is a guess written to a patient's record. **Says so**: Shutter tells you and takes no action.

Everything is played against the demo receiver or a stub listener; what a real receiver does is for its interface specification to say. "In CI" means IRIS for Health Community 2026.2, with the board's agents replaying recorded live runs; the gates, the rules and the receiver run for real.

| What goes wrong | What Shutter does | Proof | Not covered |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A transfer never reached the receiver | **Handled.** One conditional write once a person or the site's written policy releases it, checked against the receiver's receipt and history | S2 in CI | A receiver whose receipt names no digest or bed has nothing to compare |
| The receiver took the message, but its answer was lost | **Handled.** The case reads the receipt, finds it, and resends nothing | S3 in CI | A receipt under the same key with other content stops for a person. A receipt with no digest passes as it is |
| *Seen in practice.* A later transfer, or a nurse's move by hand, reaches the receiver while a resend waits for approval | **Handled.** The approval is bound to the receiver's version: when it moves, that approval is void, nothing is written on it, and the case is planned afresh from what the receiver now holds. A later transfer stands | S7, S20 in CI; [boundary check](https://github.com/MxSalata/shutter/blob/main/tests/e2e/boundary_check.py) | A change that doesn't move the version; a fix made before the case; a hand entry recorded as an update, or timed before the failed event: the new plan can propose the resend again |
| *Seen in practice.* A later move overtook the failed one, but the failed move is still owed to a system that keeps history | **Handled.** Per receiver: a current-state system closes with nothing sent; a history-of-record system goes to a person with the correction steps, never a plain replay | S22 in CI | Wards aren't compared. A failed update or discharge followed by a later transfer closes the same way today. A receiver with no declared category counts as current state |
| A transfer timed in the hour the clocks go back, on a Europe/London feed with no offset | **Stops and flags.** A time that could be either hour isn't ordered by guesswork; a person decides | S21 in CI | Europe/London only. In an outage, one such item sends its whole chain to a person (read, not played) |
| *Seen in practice.* The receiver refuses a message as "duplicate", but nothing was filed | **Handled.** That refusal closes nothing: the case reads the receipt and history, finds neither, and a person approves the resend, verified only from the receipt and history | S4 in CI; [Verify tests](https://github.com/MxSalata/shutter/blob/main/tests/unit/Shutter/Test/Verify.cls) | A receipt written before filing. A receiver that still says duplicate to the resend hasn't been played |
| A newer message for the same visit is on its way as Shutter is about to resend | **Stops and flags.** The gate and the connector hold the resend and name the message; if it lands, the case closes with nothing written | S24 in CI | The same visit only; the wait has no limit |
| A failed patient merge, perhaps since undone by hand or read the wrong way round | **Stops and flags.** Never resent automatically. By default no agent runs, the PAS team gets the case, and no reverse merge is invented | S15 in CI | Later activity and the merge's age aren't shown |
| A plain MLLP receiver answers AE, the engine suspends the message and the queue moves on. *Seen in practice:* a listener that allows one connection, so a second can knock out the live feed | **Stops and flags.** A case opens through the alert intake with the evidence and the reason; nothing is resent, and Shutter opens no MLLP connection of its own | [Plain receiver check](https://github.com/MxSalata/shutter/blob/main/tests/e2e/plain_receiver_check.py) in CI | Needs AlertOnError; every one costs a person. The test counts messages, not connections; the connection claim rests on reading the code |
| Two patients in one message, such as an A17 bed swap | **Stops and flags.** Not mapped, nothing sent, a person decides, no split invented | [Trigger test](https://github.com/MxSalata/shutter/blob/main/tests/unit/Shutter/Test/Triggers.cls), [gate test](https://github.com/MxSalata/shutter/blob/main/tests/unit/Shutter/Test/Gate.cls) | The A17 test checks the trigger, not a message with two patients. The case names the first patient only |
| Any result or order, where a resend could put a preliminary over a final | **Stops and flags.** No result or order is ever resent: up to 2 MB it gets a case and a drafted email | [Gate test](https://github.com/MxSalata/shutter/blob/main/tests/unit/Shutter/Test/Gate.cls); [onboarding check](https://github.com/MxSalata/shutter/blob/main/tests/e2e/onboard_check.py), run by hand | Over 2 MB: an alert, no case |
| "Completed" in the Message Viewer means less than it reads: reply checking off, or any positive code accepted | **Says so.** Onboarding states, per TCP operation, what Completed proves there; Verified is never read from an acknowledgement | [Onboarding check](https://github.com/MxSalata/shutter/blob/main/tests/e2e/onboard_check.py), run by hand on stock IRIS; [Verify tests](https://github.com/MxSalata/shutter/blob/main/tests/unit/Shutter/Test/Verify.cls) in CI | Batch ACKs; other adapters get a general line |
| Two approvers, one working from an old tab, or one approval clicked twice | **Handled.** In the web app a changed plan or an older case version is refused, one approval makes one write, and a closed case takes no more; in the Workflow inbox a wrong digest blocks the case with nothing written | [Boundary check](https://github.com/MxSalata/shutter/blob/main/tests/e2e/boundary_check.py) and [inbox check](https://github.com/MxSalata/shutter/blob/main/tests/e2e/inbox_check.py) in CI | Played in sequence, not at the same instant. An inbox rejection names no digest. The later person's decision isn't recorded |

## What it doesn't do yet

- It compares one visit at a time, not a patient's other visits or merged records.
- It doesn't read death or restriction fields.
- It sends an operation, not the message, so a failed A08 isn't recovered.
- Verified means this operation was in the receipt and history when read, with the digest, ward and bed they name. A closed case isn't read again.
- It takes times as stated: two sources a second apart aren't simultaneous, and a slow clock isn't noticed.
- It trusts the receiver's API: an ignored condition, a gateway's page or a partial history isn't detected.
- On the alert route it reads the alert's words and the session's first HL7 message, and doesn't see a hand resend.
- When no agent can answer, a case the catalogue gives to the board or a person goes to a person with no plan; others take the rules path, where a person still approves.
- Failover, mirrors and restores are untested, and there is no switch to stop all writes.

**Check it yourself:** the [scenarios](readme/scenarios.md) name their modes; run them with `tests/e2e/scenario_check.py --url "$IRIS_URL" --approve S4 S7 S15`, and the approval attacks with `tests/e2e/boundary_check.py --url "$IRIS_URL"`.
