# Safety case template

Headings to start your own safety case. **It is your document, and nothing here is evidence of safety.** No standard has been applied to Shutter, and no regulatory classification has been made: that depends on your intended use.

A site in England determines its deploying organisation's process under **DCB0160**, and identifies who is responsible for the manufacturer's work under **DCB0129**. Those activities belong to the organisations responsible, and neither has been carried out for Shutter. Sites elsewhere follow their own rules. Your clinical safety officer decides what applies.

1. **Intended use and excluded use.** From [the scope page](01-scope-and-exclusions.md).
2. **Deployment and organisational boundaries.** Whose system is each part; who supplies Shutter, if anyone does, and on what terms. Shutter comes with no supplier unless you arrange one.
3. **System and data-flow description.** From [the data flow page](05-data-flow-and-retention.md).
4. **People and systems relied upon.** From [the roles page](03-roles-and-handover.md), and the ordinary feed's alerting.
5. **Hazard identification and your assessment method.** Your method and your scales. [The log](04-hazard-log.csv) is a starting list, not a complete one.
6. **Claims being made.** Each specific, bounded and open to being shown wrong.
7. **Evidence for each claim.** Your own tests on your own receiver. Shutter's tests ran on a demo receiver: they show what the code did there.
8. **Assumptions and outside dependencies.** Including [each receiver answer](06-receiver-acceptance.md).
9. **Remaining hazards and acceptance decisions.** Who accepted what, and when.
10. **Operating procedures and training.** [Daily review](08-daily-review.md), approver training.
11. **Change, incident and withdrawal procedures.** [Stop and withdraw](07-stop-and-withdraw.md), tested by the people named.
12. **Review and sign-off.**
